UzSub — Privacy policy

When you sign in with Google, UzSub requests read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly) and basic profile info (name, email, picture). It reads your YouTube subscriptions, liked videos and playlists only to show them to you inside the app. It cannot modify, upload or delete anything. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties. Tokens and app data (subscriptions, watch history, playlists) are stored on the author's private server. You can revoke access anytime via the app's "Chiqish" (sign out) button or Google account third-party connections. To request deletion, email azizrixsiyev992@gmail.com.

UzSub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
